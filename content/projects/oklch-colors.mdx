## Overview

It starts with the logic. Each ramp has steps `25` to `975` and falls within a given hue range (`0` being white and `1000` black). Steps are ordered by luminance; therefore, each step corresponds to a target contrast range. Chroma is defined more loosely, but it depends on a step's relationship to its neighbors.

Since steps are aligned to luminance, we can ensure consistent contrast no matter the combination.

Colors are divided into three categories: spectrum colors (ROYGBIV & friends), neutrals, and alphas.

### Spectrum Colors

Primary and status colors for a theme. Multiple options for common status colors (red, green, yellow, etc) let you find a harmonious combo with your primary color.

### Neutral Colors

Neutrals are for icons, text, borders, and other relatively monochromatic elements. There are two informal subsets of neutrals: grays and…funky grays.

Neutral colors have a more dramatic luminance curve than the ROYGBIV ramps. This means the middle steps meant for foregrounds have a greater range of accessible pairing options. For example, the `500` step has 3:1 contrast vs. 200 instead of 100 in the saturated palettes.

## Usage

It starts with the logic. Each ramp has steps `25` to `975` and falls within a given hue range (`0` being white and `1000` black). Steps are ordered by luminance; therefore, each step corresponds to a target contrast range. Chroma is defined more loosely, but it depends on a step's relationship to its neighbors.

Ramps are based around the `500` step, which has minimum 5.2:1 contrast vs. white and 4.5:1 vs. black, a target derived from our brand teal <ColorCode value="#0d7977" />. Each multiple of 100 has minimum AA contrast with steps five spots away in either direction, and 3:1 contrast vs. those four spots away.

### Theming

With 16 hues and 8 neutrals, we can map colors to semantic slots without having to worry about conflicts.

Want your primary color to be red? _Cool, your error colors can be pink or magenta. Or ruby or orange._ Going for something more muted? _Try navy for info and gold for warnings._

Some general guidelines:

- Step 400 has a 3:1 contrast ratio vs. white through 100 and 4.5:1 vs. 900 to black.
- 500 has 5:1 contrast vs. white, 4.5:1 contrast vs. 50, and 3:1 vs. 950.
